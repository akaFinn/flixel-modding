## [FLIXEL-MODDING] - 1.6.0
# **DELAYED INDEFINITELY**

Hello everyone, Phineas here.

Wanted to write a quick note to say to everyone and to also apologize, the flixel-modding 1.6.0 update, has been indefinitely delayed.
Today was supposed to be the release day of the update, something that I had spent over 6 months refining and reworking again and again,
just to make it perfect for all of you to enjoy.

And on the morning of February 21st 2025, I had intended to upload the update however it seems as I am not as smart as I thought I was.
I tried switching to another branch only for the GitHub app to see all of my code as conflicts, nothing was building or working right. Everything got messed up.
And the me plus ChatGPT dream team wasn't helping. I had no clear path or vision and now its all gone. I hadn't updated the devlopment branch for flixel-modding in some time,
and because of that I had built up 3 months worth of content that needed to get pushed. I have no idea why my monkey brain didn't just push it before switching to the master branch.

But I didn't and now I'm here, 3 months of hard work and late night hairpulling just for it all to get deleted in front of my very eyes.
None of the changes I had made in those 3 months exist anymore, gone to the wind. The FileSystem I had built for both Native & HTML5 targets? Deleted. The stupidly complicated asset system? Dead.
All gone.

I don't think I'm going to be continuing working on this library anymore then I have, 
I've spent months of my own time I could've spent doing better things just to have it blow up in my face.
But whatever, things come and go right? 

I guess this is just a really good example of why you should always always **always** push your commit out before you do anything drastic.

Maybe one day I'll get back on my ass and remake all those systems again, I don't know who cares.
If you want to use the latest beta for flixel-modding you can find it on github, however I will not be uploading it to Haxelib.
If you also want to maybe reimplement the lost features yourself, you can find a file in the development branch called `intended-features.txt`
and if some brave soul out there actually attempts to recover what is lost, then I'll push it to the master branch myself and give you a crisp high-five.

Anyhoo, I do not plan to continue developing for this library anymore, sorry but it's just not worth it anymore.
Had a lot of fun doing it however, learning new things is always great. Again sorry for hyping it up just for it go away forever, it stinks.

I'll see you all around I suppose, maybe I'll even make more libraries.
Thank you for understanding, and again I apologize.

Goodbye.
